FAQ

Questions parents and students actually ask.

How does the 3 free monthly submissions work?
Every account gets 3 free story or essay submissions each calendar month — full Key Takeaways feedback included, no watered-down version. Your count resets automatically at the start of each billing cycle, so there's nothing to remember or manually renew. Once you're using it every week, Pro removes the monthly cap entirely.
Is my child's writing safe and private?
Yes. Submissions are processed solely to generate feedback for your child — we never sell or rent writing samples or personal data, and we don't use your child's submissions to train public AI models. Parents can view, export, or permanently delete a child's writing history at any time. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
How does LiteracyLab AI align with my country's curriculum?
You choose your country — UK, US, Australia, or Canada — and your child's grade or year group, and every mastery target, growth tip, and vocabulary suggestion is scaled to that curriculum's actual expectations (e.g. fronted adverbials for UK Key Stage 2, rhetorical devices for US high-school English). It's not one generic rubric stretched across every country.
Can I cancel my LiteracyLab Pro subscription anytime?
Yes — Pro is billed monthly through Stripe with no lock-in contract. Cancel from your account settings in a couple of clicks and you won't be charged again; you'll keep Pro access through the end of the period you already paid for.
What happens when my child reaches their 3-story limit?
The workspace clearly shows when they're at the limit and pauses new submissions — nothing is lost, and drafts already in progress stay saved. From there you can either upgrade to Pro for unlimited submissions right away, or simply wait for the free allowance to reset at the start of next month.
