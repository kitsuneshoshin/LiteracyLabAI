Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 September 2026 · Last updated: 15 September 2026
Prototype notice: LiteracyLab AI is currently an interactive product prototype. This document is a working draft written to reflect our intended data practices under COPPA, UK/EU GDPR (including the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, sometimes referred to as "GDPR-K"), and general international data-protection standards. It has not been reviewed by qualified privacy counsel and must be finalized by a licensed attorney before the Service processes any real child's personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how LiteracyLab AI ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when a parent or legal guardian ("Account Holder", "you") creates an account and sets up a profile for their child or student ("Child User") on our website and workspace (the "Service").

1. Who manages the account

The Service is built around parental control by design. A parent or legal guardian creates the account, provides billing information, and creates and manages every Child User profile linked to that account. Child Users do not register independently, do not provide their own contact information, and cannot change account-level settings such as billing, subscription plan, or profile deletion — only the Account Holder can do this.

Because many Child Users are under 13, we obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a Child User, consistent with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("COPPA"). Consent is obtained from the Account Holder at the point the account and any Child User profile is created.

2. Information we collect

CategoryWhat we collectFrom whom

We do not knowingly collect a Child User's precise geolocation, contact information (email, phone), government identifiers, or biometric data, and we do not require any of this to use the Service.

3. How submissions are processed by AI — in plain terms

When a Child User submits a story, essay, or a set of reading-comprehension answers, that content is sent securely to a third-party large language model provider (for example, OpenAI's API) solely to generate real-time educational feedback — a Glow, a Grow, vocabulary suggestions, and a micro-mission. This processing happens through the provider's business/API terms, under which:

  • submitted text is not sold, rented, or otherwise disclosed to any third party for their own marketing or commercial purposes;
  • submitted text is not used to train the provider's general-purpose public AI models; and
  • the provider acts only as a data processor carrying out our instructions, not as an independent owner of the data.

We do not use Child User writing submissions for behavioral advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles of Child Users.

4. How we use information

  • To create and operate parent and child accounts, and to generate the feedback the Service exists to provide;
  • To track a Child User's mastery progress over time and display it to the Account Holder on the parent dashboard;
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions;
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and fair use of the Service (see our Terms of Use); and
  • To communicate with the Account Holder about their account, billing, or material changes to our policies.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use Child User data for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are used under applicable US state privacy laws.

5. Your rights as a parent or guardian

At any time, an Account Holder may:

  • Review the personal information and writing history collected for any Child User on their account, from the parent dashboard;
  • Export a copy of a Child User's saved writing history and profile data in a portable format, on request;
  • Correct inaccurate profile information (e.g. grade level, display name);
  • Delete a specific Child User's profile and associated writing history, or delete the entire account, at any time; and
  • Withdraw consent for further collection of a Child User's information, which will result in that profile being deactivated.

To exercise any of these rights, use the relevant control in account settings, or email privacy@literacylab.ai. We will act on deletion and export requests without unreasonable delay, and in any case within the timeframe required by applicable law.

6. UK and EU users (GDPR and the Children's Code)

For users in the UK and EU, our lawful basis for processing a Child User's personal data is parental consent together with the performance of our contract with the Account Holder. In addition to the rights in Section 5, UK/EU Account Holders have the right to restrict or object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

In line with the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the Service applies privacy-protective defaults for Child Users: profile visibility is private by default, no geolocation is collected, no "nudge" techniques are used to encourage additional data disclosure or extended usage beyond what a parent has configured, and no options that weaken a child's privacy are enabled by default.

7. Data retention

We retain account and Child User data for as long as the account remains active, plus a limited period afterward to allow for account recovery and to meet legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations. Upon a verified deletion request, we delete or irreversibly de-identify the associated personal data and writing submissions within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Data security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and restricted internal access to Child User data — to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, and we will notify affected Account Holders in the event of a data breach as required by applicable law.

9. International data transfers

Because our infrastructure and AI-processing providers may operate in countries other than your own, personal data may be transferred internationally. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses for UK/EU transfers) to ensure data receives an equivalent level of protection wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes affecting how we handle Child User data will be communicated to Account Holders via the email on file before they take effect.

11. Contact us

Questions, deletion requests, or export requests can be sent to privacy@literacylab.ai.
