How to use a prompt

  • Let your child read the prompt aloud and say what they would write before they start.
  • Keep it short and regular. One carefully improved piece teaches more than three rushed ones.
  • Praise one specific thing they did well, then pick one thing to improve next time.

Make it count

A prompt is the start. The real learning comes from feedback on what a child actually wrote. Our year-by-year guides explain what children are working on at each age, so you know what to look for.
